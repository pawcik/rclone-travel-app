Privacy policy for rclone travel sync

Last updated: 25 October 2026

What the app accesses

rclone travel sync accesses only the owner's own Google Drive, through the Google Drive API. It uses this access to copy files from the owner's machine into one Drive folder.

Storage of credentials

OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's machine.

Data collection and sharing

No data is collected, shared, sold or sent to third parties.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
